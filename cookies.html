Overview

This Cookie Policy applies to the Lunera website and should be read together with our Privacy Policy .

Cookies and similar browser technologies can be used to remember information about a visit, maintain website functionality or store user preferences.

Current website setup

The current Lunera website stores your cookie-card preference locally so the same notice does not need to appear on every visit.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website may ask a browser to store. Similar technologies, including local browser storage, can also remember preferences between visits.

Some technologies are necessary for basic functionality, while others may support analytics, advertising or additional features where enabled.

Essential storage

Essential browser storage may be used where necessary for basic website behaviour or to remember a choice made by the visitor.

Cookie preference

Essential

Stores whether the cookie notice was accepted or declined so it does not repeatedly cover the page.

Website functionality

Essential

May be used where technically necessary to maintain basic website operation or security.

Your cookie preference

When the cookie notice is shown, you can choose to accept or decline. The selected option is saved in browser storage under a website-specific preference key.

Saving this preference does not require you to provide your name, email address or other contact information.

Compact consent card

The website uses a small horizontal notice designed to remain unobtrusive while still giving visitors a clear cookie choice.

Optional cookies

Optional cookies may be introduced in the future if additional analytics, advertising or embedded services are added to the website.

Where consent is legally required, optional cookies should not be activated before the appropriate choice has been obtained.

Analytics

If enabled

Could help understand how visitors interact with pages and which content is useful.

Marketing

If enabled

Could support advertising, campaign measurement or audience-related functionality.

Embedded media

If enabled

External media services may store their own information when embedded content is used.

Preferences

If enabled

Could remember non-essential interface or content preferences selected by the visitor.

Third-party services

The website may use external services for areas such as hosting, fonts, media or other technical functionality.

Third-party providers may operate under their own privacy and cookie policies. Their practices are controlled by those providers rather than Lunera.

If additional third-party services are introduced, this policy may be updated to reflect the relevant cookie behaviour.

Managing cookies and storage

Most modern browsers provide controls for viewing, deleting or restricting cookies and other stored website data.

  • You may delete stored website data through your browser settings.
  • You may block some or all cookies through browser privacy controls.
  • Clearing browser storage may cause the cookie notice to appear again.
  • Blocking essential storage may affect some website functionality.
Resetting the website choice

If local website data is removed from your browser, the stored Lunera cookie preference will also be removed and can be selected again.

Storage duration

The duration for which cookies or browser storage remain available depends on the technology used, browser settings and whether the visitor manually removes stored data.

Essential preferences should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the intended website functionality.

Changes to this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated if website functionality changes, new services are introduced or applicable requirements change.

The current version may be published on this page together with the latest update date.
