Your information should be handled clearly and responsibly.

This policy describes the types of information that may be processed through this website and the general purposes for which that information may be used.

Introduction

This website is operated by Lunera, an independent marketing agency. References to “Lunera”, “we”, “us” or “our” in this policy refer to the operator of this website.

By using this website, you acknowledge that information may be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements.

Information we collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit a project enquiry or otherwise communicate through the website.

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • The service you are interested in.
  • Information included in your project enquiry or message.
  • Technical information that may automatically be generated when accessing a website, such as basic browser or server request data.
No phone numbers are requested

The website enquiry forms are designed around email communication and do not request a telephone number.

How we use information

Information provided through the website may be used where reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, discuss potential projects and operate the website.

  • To respond to your enquiry.
  • To understand the service or project you are interested in.
  • To communicate about a potential or existing business relationship.
  • To maintain the security and functionality of the website.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

Contact forms

When you submit a form through this website, the information entered into that form may be transmitted to us by email so that we can review and respond to your enquiry.

Please avoid including information that is not reasonably necessary for discussing your project or request.

Email communication

If you send an enquiry, we may use the email address you provide to reply to that enquiry and continue relevant project communication.

Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar browser storage where necessary to provide basic functionality and remember certain website preferences.

More information about the way cookies are handled on this website is available in our Cookie Policy .

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Information may only be shared where reasonably necessary for operating the website, providing services or meeting legal obligations.

This may include service providers involved in website hosting, email delivery or related technical services, subject to appropriate arrangements where required.

Data retention

Personal information is generally retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining relevant business records or meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the communication, business relationship and applicable requirements.

Your rights

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where permitted.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Rights are subject to applicable law and may not apply in every circumstance.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures may be used to protect information handled through the website against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

No method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the website, services, legal requirements or the way information is handled.

When changes are made, the updated version may be published on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or the way information is handled through this website, you can contact us by email.
